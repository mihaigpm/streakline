# Compliance guidance — v1.0

## Age rating and alcohol-related content

Do not assert or select an age rating from this document. Complete Apple's
current age-rating questionnaire truthfully based on the submitted binary and
listing.

Pay particular attention to questions about:

- References to or use of alcohol
- Health, wellness, or medical information
- User-generated content (none is intended in v1.0)
- Gambling, contests, unrestricted web access, violence, and sexual content
  (none is intended in v1.0)

The app tracks user-entered alcohol consumption but does not sell alcohol,
facilitate purchases, encourage intoxication, or provide social sharing. The
privacy policy states that the app is intended for adults of legal drinking age
and is not directed at children. These facts should inform truthful
questionnaire answers; they do not predetermine the rating Apple assigns.

Recheck the questionnaire whenever content or functionality changes.

## Medical and wellness declarations

- Describe Streakline as a habit and fitness tracker.
- Do not claim diagnosis, treatment, prevention, clinical efficacy, or a
  medically safe drinking threshold.
- Keep the non-medical disclaimer in the listing and app/website support copy.
- Direct people concerned about alcohol use or withdrawal to qualified
  healthcare professionals or appropriate local support.
- Do not present the automatic budget as personalised medical guidance.

## Export compliance

The project declares `ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption` as `false`. Before
submission, verify the archived binary uses only encryption exempt from export
documentation (for example, encryption supplied by the operating system) and
answer App Store Connect's export-compliance questions to match the binary.
Do not rely solely on the project setting if networking or security libraries
are added.
