**Description**

Streakline brings drinking-less goals and a simple fitness plan into one private
habit tracker.

Choose how you count drinks—pints, UK units, or standard drinks—and set your
starting weekly budget. Your target drops by 1 every two weeks until it reaches
5, giving you a clear, gradual path to follow.

Train three days a week with a built-in A/B/C workout plan. Each exercise
includes instructions, form cues, common mistakes, and a focused step-by-step
view.

Stay motivated as you:

- Log drinks and see alcohol-free days at a glance
- Complete guided workouts and individual exercises
- Earn XP, unlock badges, and climb through nine ranks
- Build weekly streaks by completing workouts and staying within budget
- Receive optional reminders scheduled locally on your iPhone

Streakline requires no account. Your drinks, workouts, streaks, and progress are
stored locally on your device and are not sent to Streakline servers.

Streakline is a habit and fitness tracker, not a medical device or a substitute
for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. If you are concerned
about alcohol use or withdrawal, contact a qualified healthcare professional or
an appropriate local support service.
