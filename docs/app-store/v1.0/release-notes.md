# Version 1.0 release notes

Welcome to Streakline.

- Choose your drink unit and starting weekly budget
- Follow a target that drops by 1 every two weeks to a floor of 5
- Work through three guided workouts each week
- Track drinks and alcohol-free days
- Earn XP, unlock badges, climb ranks, and build weekly streaks
- Set optional reminders that are scheduled on your iPhone

No account is required, and your tracking data stays on your device.
