# App Review notes — v1.0

Streakline is an iPhone-only habit and fitness tracker. It combines a fixed
three-day weekly workout plan with user-entered drink tracking.

## Access

- No account, sign-in, subscription, or purchase is required.
- No demo credentials are needed.
- The app has no backend dependency.
- Network access is not required for core functionality.
- Notification permission is optional and may be declined.

## Suggested review path

1. Launch the app and complete onboarding.
2. Choose a drink unit and a starting weekly budget.
3. On Home, use the plus and minus controls to change the current week's drink
   total.
4. Open a workout, review exercise instructions, mark exercises complete, and
   complete the workout.
5. Open Progress to view XP, rank, streak, and badge progress.
6. Open Settings to change the drink unit or optional reminder preference.

The weekly drink target decreases by 1 every two weeks until it reaches a floor
of 5. This progression may not be visible during a short review session because
it is based on app weeks.

## Health and safety context

The app does not diagnose, treat, or provide medical advice. It does not claim
that its budget is clinically appropriate. Users choose their starting budget,
and the app presents habit-tracking and fitness features. The listing directs
people with concerns about alcohol use or withdrawal to qualified professional
or local support.

## Data handling

User-entered drinks, workouts, dry days, settings, XP, badges, and streaks are
stored locally on the device. There are no user accounts, analytics SDKs,
advertising SDKs, or Streakline servers. Optional reminders are scheduled
locally through iOS.
